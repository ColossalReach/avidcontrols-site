Why Bundles Work

You Used to Need 5 Vendors. Now You Need One.

Sourcing Smarts from one distributor and Parts from another means two lead times, two freight shipments, and two sets of problems to solve if anything goes wrong.

Designed Together

Every bundle is engineered as a complete system — the control layer is sized and configured for the physical layer. No mismatches. No compatibility surprises on commissioning day.

Tested Together

The full system runs a 48-hour burn-test as one unit — Smarts driving Parts, end-to-end signal flow verified, control commands confirmed. If it fails here, we fix it before it leaves.

Shipped Together

The Margin Math on a Bundle

Sourcing Smarts and Parts separately: two distributors, two freight bills, two lead time risks, two staging days, 8–12 hours of baseline programming. A bundle eliminates all of that. The cost of the bundle is the cost of the components minus your staging labor, programming time, and second freight charge. The math works — and your crew stays on the jobsite where they're billable.
